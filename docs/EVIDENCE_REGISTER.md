# KAFMAF evidence register

Research date: 9 October 2026. This register separates what is independently verifiable, what was
supplied by people connected to KAFMAF, and what is unknown. **Nothing in sections B–D should be
treated as approved public content until KAFMAF leadership confirms it.** All of it can be entered
(or left blank) in **Admin → Settings & content**; blank fields are hidden on the public site.
